About us

A window, an oven and a lot of hungry surfers

The Saucy Seagull started as a tiny slice window on the Ocean City boardwalk, run by two lifeguards who were tired of eating sad sandwiches on their breaks. Ten summers later, we still hand-stretch every pie, make the sauce fresh every morning and bake it hot and fast in our wood-fired oven.

Grab a slice at the window, take a whole pie to the sand, or sit at the counter and watch the waves. Wet hair and flip-flops are the dress code.

  • Hand-stretched dough
  • Sauce made daily
  • Wood-fired, 90 seconds

Where to eat
your slice

Our favorite spots within a few minutes' walk of the window. Bring napkins. Keep one eye on the sky.

  • 4 min walk

    By the Music Pier

    Grab a boardwalk bench by the Music Pier and eat with an ocean view and a free summer concert.

    Tip: the wind picks up in the afternoon. Hold your plate.

  • 5 min walk

    The lifeguard stand

    Set up near a lifeguard stand on the beach, the perfect backdrop for a slice photo.

    Tip: don't forget your beach tag.

  • 1 min walk

    Straight to the sand

    Walk out the door, kick off your sandals and spread a towel. Easiest picnic in town.

    Tip: ask for a whole pie in a beach box.

  • 12 min walk

    Sunset on the bay

    Walk across the island to the bayside and watch the sun set over the water with a slice in hand.

    Tip: order 30 minutes before sunset.
